```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A["Drag a file"] --> B["Press your shortcut"] --> C["Drop it on the wheel"] --> D["Done"]
```

## What it can do

- **Convert** files: PDF to Word, pictures to PDF, and more.
- **Compress** to any size, like "under 200 KB".
- **Edit** and **crop** pictures and PDFs.
- **Remove location data** from your photos.
- **Word, PowerPoint and Excel to PDF.**

## Windows says "Windows protected your PC"?

Click **More info** → **Run anyway**. Spindle isn't signed yet; the message is about that, not about the app.
